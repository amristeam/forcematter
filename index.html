Key equations

  • F = kxHooke's law, k in N m−1
  • E = ½Fx = ½kx²elastic energy, within the limit
  • 1/k = 1/k₁ + 1/k₂springs in series
  • k = k₁ + k₂springs in parallel
  • σ = F/A, ε = x/Lstress and strain
  • p = F/Apressure, Pa = N m−2
  • p = ρghpressure due to a liquid column
